## out of scope (per vision)

- **`DomainObjectPragmaRef` / `x-domain-object-ref`** (open-q8, cross-team): the ref pragma
  `{ of, by }` is a peer branch's concern. this execution ships only the **base** pragma
  (`DomainObjectPragma`) + `kind`. the base-name choice (`DomainObjectPragma`, unmarked) was
  made so the ref partner lands coherently, but the ref stamp is not built here.
- **a fill/parse transformer** (open-q5): dropped — with optional keys there is no field to
  fill, and the node-read is a plain property access the consumer owns.

---

## the "aha" — what shipped

- **exactness**: `kind` comes from the subclass marker, the source of truth — not the fragile
  `primary`-non-empty heuristic. kills both misclassification directions (literal-with-primary,
  entity-without-primary).
- **shared vocabulary**: one exported `DomainObjectPragma` type; consumers delete their local
  hand-rolled copy.
- **additive**: `kind` is a new pragma field; a consumer's inference fallback still fires until
  it upgrades. honest caveat — exact-equality assertions + snapshots break (updated here).
