# 🌙 dream: the achiever's route ledger is trackable, and leaks verbatim asks into public repos

- kind: reseed
- owner: ehmpathy/rhachet-roles-bhrain (achiever role)
- caught 2026-11-21

## .the cue that fired

on domain-objects pr #144, the commit was refused by the new `check.timestamps.sh` pre-commit check: a clock time sat in the route's `.goals/asks.inventory.jsonl`, the achiever's verbatim ledger of human asks.

## .the gap, precisely

- the achiever writes each human ask, verbatim, into `$route/.goals/`
- no `.gitignore` covers that dir, so `git.commit.set --unstaged include` stages it
- the ledger holds the wisher's words: operational orders, names, times. in a public repo that ships to every clone
- `goal.guard` forbids any direct touch of `.goals/`, so a repo cannot add its own ignore entry there, and a driver cannot unstage it (a human had to, via `git.crew.unstage`)
- the route's own `.route/.gitignore` already shows the pattern: `ignore all except passage.jsonl and .bind flags`

## .the shape of the fix

- achiever ships `$route/.goals/.gitignore` with `*` (or `*` + `!.gitignore`) on first write, as the driver does for `.route/`
- or, if the ledger is meant to be tracked: mask clock times on write, and record that the asks are public

## .why it is not done in this round

the fix belongs to the achiever role in another repo; `goal.guard` blocks a local fix by design.
